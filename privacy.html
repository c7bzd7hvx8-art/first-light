First Light Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

The short version: First Light's core features do not require or collect personal data. Your location is used only on your device to calculate legal shooting times. If you use the optional Cull Diary, your records are stored securely in Supabase under your account and protected by access controls. Only you can access your personal diary data, except what you deliberately share with a syndicate — shared entries, plus the shared-ground maps, seats, bookings and field notes described below. We use privacy-focused page-view statistics (GoatCounter) only to see aggregate traffic across the First Light site and app — not for advertising or profiling. We do not serve ads or sell data.

What we collect

Core app (Species, Legal Times, Calendar, Field Guide, Deer School, Ballistics Calculator) — no personal data collected:

  • No account required
  • Anonymous page-view statistics via GoatCounter (aggregate traffic only) — see Third-party services
  • No cookies placed on your device by First Light
  • No advertising identifiers collected
  • The Ballistics Calculator keeps the rifle and load profiles you build — calibre, bullet weight, ballistic coefficient, muzzle velocity, sight height, zero range — in your browser on this device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and no account is needed to use it. If it fetches current weather for you, that is the same anonymous Open-Meteo call described below.

Cull Diary (optional) — requires an account:

  • Email address and password for authentication
  • Cull records you enter (species, date, location, weights, photos, notes)
  • Live-deer sightings you log (species, approximate numbers seen, behaviour, and an optional location and photo) — private to your account, never shared to a syndicate
  • Saved stands / high seats you create for the wind forecast (a name, a map location, look directions, and bad-wind sectors) — private to your account unless you are in a syndicate with a shared ground: members of that syndicate can then see each seat's name, ground, map location, look directions and bad-wind sectors on the shared map and booking planner, so the group can plan and book around them. A stand's private notes are never shared.
  • Shared ground maps: if you share a mapped ground with a syndicate, its boundary, no-shoot zones, lines and markers become visible to that syndicate's members. Your culls and diary entries on that ground do not.
  • Bookings you make on a shared ground (the seat or ground, date, dawn/dusk window, guest flag, and any note you add), stored with your display name and visible to that syndicate's members; you can export your own bookings as a calendar file
  • Ground field notes you mark as Shared, visible to members on that ground's sheet; notes left Private stay yours alone
  • Push notifications (optional): if you turn the notification bell on, your browser creates a push subscription — an endpoint address plus delivery keys for this device — which is stored in Supabase so booking requests, approvals and announcements can reach you. Turning the bell off, or deleting your account, deletes that subscription. Delivery is relayed by your browser vendor's push service — see Third-party services.
  • Basic error diagnostics: if something goes wrong, a technical report (the page address, your browser type, and the error message and stack trace) is sent to our Supabase database so we can fix bugs; while you are signed in it includes your account ID. It contains no diary content, and any sign-in tokens are stripped from the address before it is sent.
  • Diary data is stored securely in Supabase and accessible by you; entries you intentionally share to a syndicate are also accessible to relevant syndicate members
  • Syndicate chat messages you post (up to 500 characters), stored with your display name and role at the time of posting; messages are visible to that syndicate's members and managers
  • Removing a chat message hides it from the thread (shown as "message removed") but the message record is retained in the database; messages you posted remain in the syndicate's thread, with the display name captured at time of posting, after you leave the syndicate or delete your account
  • You can delete any entry or your entire account at any time

Your location

If you grant location permission, your GPS coordinates are used on your device only to calculate sunrise, sunset, and legal shooting times. This calculation happens entirely within your browser.

First Light does not operate its own server, so your coordinates are not sent to us. Two external calls may be made with location data: an anonymous request to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to reverse-geocode your nearest place name, and an anonymous request to Open-Meteo to fetch the weather for your area. Both are described below.

You can also set your location manually using the search or preset locations, in which case no GPS data is used at all.

Cull Diary entries with a GPS pin: if you choose to attach a location to a cull entry (either by dropping a map pin or by granting GPS access inside the diary form), those coordinates are stored with the entry in Supabase under your account. Map tiles for that pin are loaded from the tile providers listed below. You can remove the pin from any entry, or delete the entry entirely.

Location inside a photo (EXIF): GPS permission is not the only way a location can reach an entry. A photo taken on a phone often carries the coordinates and the time of capture embedded in the image file itself. When you attach a photo, First Light reads those two facts on your device, in your browser, and offers them to you on a small card under the photo. Nothing is applied until you tap to accept: if you do, the coordinate is stored with the entry and reverse-geocoded to a place name exactly as a dropped pin would be, and the capture time fills in the date and time fields. Tap dismiss and the reading is discarded. The original file is never uploaded.

The photo we do upload carries no metadata at all. Before a photo leaves your device it is re-drawn onto a canvas and re-encoded as a fresh JPEG. That step discards every metadata block the original held — GPS coordinates, capture time, camera make, model and serial number. The image stored in Supabase therefore has no location embedded in it, whatever the original had, and this is deliberate rather than incidental. The only location ever attached to an entry is one you chose to attach.

Third-party services

First Light is a static site plus a managed database; everything else comes from external providers. When your browser loads a page or a map, it will contact the following services directly. In each case the only information the provider sees is what any website contact reveals — your IP address, browser user-agent, and (for map and weather calls) a coordinate.

Used by all features:

GoatCounter
A small script from gc.zgo.at records which site pages are viewed and rough traffic levels so we can see how busy the site and app are. It is not used for advertising. goatcounter.com/help/privacy
jsDelivr & cdnjs (CDNs)
Third-party JavaScript libraries (the Supabase client and jsPDF) are loaded from the jsDelivr and cdnjs content delivery networks. These CDNs see the request IP address but receive no app data. jsdelivr.com/privacy · cdnjs.com
unpkg (CDN)
The Leaflet marker-clustering library (one script and two stylesheets) is loaded from the unpkg content delivery network. Like the CDNs above, it sees the request IP address but receives no app data. unpkg.com

Used by the core app (Species, Legal Times, Calendar, Field Guide, Ballistics Calculator):

Open-Meteo
Weather forecasts. Your approximate coordinates are sent to fetch weather data. Open-Meteo is a free, open-source weather API that does not require an account or store personal data. open-meteo.com
OpenStreetMap Nominatim
Place name lookup for reverse geocoding your coordinates. Requests are anonymous; OSM is a non-profit. nominatim.org · OSMF privacy
timeapi.io · worldtimeapi.org
Authoritative UK-local time lookups used to stamp your diary entries correctly across time-zone boundaries (BST / GMT). Both are free, public APIs; requests carry no personal data beyond the request IP. timeapi.io · worldtimeapi.org

Used by the Cull Diary (optional — requires an account):

Supabase
Your account (email and password), cull entries, photos, and syndicate memberships are stored in Supabase — a GDPR-compliant managed cloud database. Data is stored under your user ID, protected by row-level security, and only accessible to you (and, for shared syndicate culls, to other members of your syndicate). Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf. supabase.com/privacy
Mapbox
Map tiles for the cull map and the entry-form pin picker. Mapbox sees your IP address, user-agent, and the map coordinates being viewed, and collects aggregate usage telemetry via events.mapbox.com. No personal First Light data is shared with Mapbox. mapbox.com/legal/privacy
Ordnance Survey (OS Maps API)
Optional OS Road raster map tiles for UK-centred map views, via api.os.uk. OS sees your IP address and the tile coordinates being requested. ordnancesurvey.co.uk/privacy
Esri / ArcGIS World Imagery
Satellite tile fallback when Mapbox is unavailable, via server.arcgisonline.com. Esri sees your IP address and the tile coordinates requested. esri.com/privacy
OpenStreetMap tile servers
OSM standard raster tiles as a further fallback, via tile.openstreetmap.org. OSM sees your IP address and the tile coordinates requested. OSMF privacy

Field Guide — partner organisation logos:

firstlightdeer.co.uk (logo image files)
The Field Guide shows small logo images for UK Deer Track & Recovery (UKDTR), the British Deer Society (BDS), and BASC on informational cards, with links to each organisation's own website. Those image files are served from firstlightdeer.co.uk. If you follow a link to an organisation's site, that organisation's privacy policy applies there.
Browser push services (Google, Mozilla, Apple)
Only if you turn notifications on in the diary. Web push is delivered by the push service built into your browser — Google's for Chrome and Android browsers, Mozilla's for Firefox, Apple's for Safari. That service handles your device's push endpoint and an encrypted payload; the notification content is prepared by our Supabase function and encrypted to your device. Turning the bell off deletes the subscription from our database.

The names and logos of UKDTR, BDS, and BASC are trademarks of their respective owners. They are shown only to identify those organisations and point you to official resources; display does not imply that First Light is endorsed by them.

Data storage

First Light keeps a number of small values in your browser's localStorage. All of them stay on your device, are readable only by First Light pages, and can be wiped at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this site. None of them is sent to us — First Light has no server to send them to. This is the complete list.

Preferences and screen state

  • fl_state — your last-used location (coordinates and place name) and which tab you were on
  • firstlight_disclaimer_seen, fl-ballistics-accepted-v1, fl-species-onboarded-v1 — whether you have already seen the legal disclaimer, the calculator's acceptance notice, and the species-picker introduction
  • fl_field_mode, fl-my-species-v1, fl-cull-layer, fl-stands-compact, fl-stands-cones-off, fl-stnd-names-v1, fl-stnd-mk-names-v1, fl-export-seats-v1, fl-plan-open, fl-syndicate-open, fl-last-ground-v1 — low-light mode, your species shortlist, map-versus-satellite choice, which map labels and overlays you have switched on, and which panels you last had open
  • fl_uk_clock_offset_ms, fl_uk_clock_synced_at_ms — the measured difference between your device clock and UK time, so entries and legal shooting times are stamped correctly
  • fl_debug — a diagnostic flag, off unless you deliberately turn it on

Ballistics Calculator — no account, and nothing here leaves the device

  • fl-ballistics-profiles-v1 — the rifle and load profiles you create: calibre, bullet weight, ballistic coefficient, muzzle velocity, sight height and zero range
  • fl-ballistics-settings-v1 — which profile is active, your units, jurisdiction and species filter, the anatomy-overlay and night-mode settings, and the range and atmospheric conditions you last dialled in, so the calculator survives your phone going to sleep

Deer School

  • fl_groundschool — your quiz progress and score history, kept on the device so the learning dashboard works without an account

Cull Diary — on-device working copies of your own data

  • fl-grounds-cache-v1, fl-stands-cache-v1 — your grounds and the high seats you have saved, cached so the map and the wind forecast open instantly and still work with no signal
  • fl-stands-forecast-v4 — recent weather forecasts for those locations, cached so the app does not refetch them every time
  • fl-score-log-v1 — a local log of what the wind forecast predicted against what you actually recorded, used only to show you how well the forecast has been doing
  • fl_offline_queue with its -corrupt and -deadletter companions, and fl_offline_synced_recent — entries you created with no signal, waiting to upload, plus a short record of what has just synced so nothing uploads twice
  • fl_synd_msg_seen:… — the timestamp of the last syndicate message you read, per syndicate, so the unread badge is right
  • fl-shared-grounds-cache-v1 — the shared-ground maps and members' seats your syndicates have shared with you, cached so the shared map works offline
  • fl-stand-outbox-v1 and its -deadletter companion — stand edits made with no signal, waiting to upload
  • fl_bookings_cache:…, fl_ground_bridge:…, fl_syn_last:…, fl_synset_open, fl_syn_tell, fl_outing_nudge_dismissed:… — cached booking rows for your syndicate's grounds, which syndicate and ground you last had open, whether the settings card was folded, your share-to-syndicate default, and booking nudges you have dismissed
  • fl_pending_invite — a syndicate invite link you opened before signing in, held only until it is redeemed or you sign out
  • fl-home-ground-card-v1 — the home page's "your ground" card summary (ground name, seat count, best seat)
  • fl-gmap-draft-v1 — an unsaved ground-map drawing, so an interrupted drawing session can resume
  • fl-units — your metres/yards and kilograms/pounds display choice
  • fl_map_load_est_… and its two warning flags — a running count of how many map tiles this browser has loaded this month, so we can tell whether the free map allowance is close to running out. It is a single number; it is not a record of where you looked.
  • The Supabase authentication token, a key beginning sb-, which keeps you signed in between sessions. It never leaves your device except when talking to Supabase.

If you use the Cull Diary, your cull records and photos themselves are stored in Supabase under your account, associated with your email address and protected by row-level security — only you can read your own entries. The diary also uses an on-device IndexedDB store to hold photos attached to entries you created while offline, so nothing is lost when signal is poor; once you reconnect, those entries and photos are uploaded to Supabase and removed from the device.

Signing out, or deleting your account, clears the diary's on-device data from this browser: the grounds and stands caches, the shared-ground cache, the cached forecasts, the forecast-accuracy log, your species shortlist and last-used ground, your saved home location, the offline queues — entries and stand edits alike, so sync before signing out or unsynced work is discarded — cached bookings and syndicate state, any un-redeemed invite, and the offline photos in IndexedDB. Your general preferences and your ballistics profiles are deliberately left alone, so signing out does not cost you your rifle setup.

No data is stored on any First Light server at any point — First Light does not operate a server of its own.

Data retention

Core app: nothing is retained on any server of ours, because there is no server of ours. The only thing that persists is the on-device localStorage set out in full under Data storage above, which stays on your device until you clear your browser's site data.

Cull Diary: your account and diary data are retained for as long as your account exists. You can:

  • Delete an individual cull entry at any time — it is removed immediately from Supabase (including any attached photos).
  • Delete your entire account at any time, using the Delete my account & all data button in the diary. This triggers a server-side procedure that immediately removes all of your cull entries, sightings, stands, their photos, your mapped grounds and their features, your bookings, any push subscriptions, syndicate memberships, and the Supabase auth account itself. No personal "soft-delete" or recovery copy of your identifiable data is kept. The one exception is syndicate tallies: culls you attributed to a syndicate are retained in anonymised form (species, sex and date only, with no link back to you) so the group's season totals stay accurate after you leave or delete your account.

Backups held by Supabase as part of its normal database operations may retain deleted records for a short period before being overwritten; see Supabase's own retention terms.

Error diagnostics are currently kept without a fixed time limit. They contain no diary content and no location, and when you delete your account each stored report keeps only its technical content — the link to your account is removed.

Your rights under UK GDPR

First Light is operated from the United Kingdom and subject to the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The core app collects no personal data, so most of these rights apply only to the Cull Diary.

If you use the Cull Diary, your account email and diary entries are processed on the lawful basis of consent — you choose to create an account and enter data. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account. Notifications are separately opt-in (the bell control in the diary) and can be withdrawn at any time the same way — turning the bell off deletes the subscription. You have the right to:

  • Access your data — it is already visible to you in the app; on request we can confirm what is held.
  • Rectify incorrect data — edit any entry directly, or contact us.
  • Erase your data — delete individual entries or your entire account in-app (see Data retention above).
  • Portability — export your diary from the Stats tab as CSV, PDF, Season Summary, and Larder Book; your sightings log as CSV or PDF; any mapped ground as GeoJSON or GPX; and your bookings as a calendar (.ics) file (season and permission/ground scoped where the export asks for it). Photos referenced on entries remain in secure cloud storage.
  • Restrict or object to further processing — stop using the diary, or delete your account.
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been mishandled: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

International transfers

First Light is operated from the UK, but some of the providers above are global networks: the content delivery networks, the map-tile providers and — if you enable notifications — your browser vendor's push service may process the limited data described above on servers outside the UK. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by each provider's own UK GDPR-compliant terms (UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses, as their policies set out). Your diary database itself is held by Supabase in the project's hosting region and is not moved as part of normal operation.

Children

First Light is intended for adults involved in or learning about legal deer management in the UK. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from minors.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that. Most changes affect the optional Cull Diary processing and third-party providers listed above; core non-account features remain privacy-light.
