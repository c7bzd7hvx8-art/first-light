2. What First Light is (and isn't)

First Light is a set of digital tools for UK deer stalkers — legal shooting-time calculators, species information, seasonal calendars, a learning dashboard (Deer School), a Ballistics Calculator, and the optional Cull Diary for recording your own cull activity. It is intended as a practical aid, not as legal, veterinary, food-hygiene, firearms or ballistic advice.

The Ballistics Calculator is a planning aid, and nothing more. It models a trajectory from figures you supply, or from published manufacturer data measured in a test barrel that is not your barrel. Everything it produces — come-ups, wind holds, retained velocity and energy, the statutory-minimum pass or fail indication, and the "ethical maximum range" — is calculated guidance. None of it substitutes for chronographing your own load, zeroing your own rifle, and confirming the fall of shot on a range. Statutory calibre, bullet-weight and muzzle-energy minimums differ between England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, and they change; the compliance indication is our reading of those rules, not legal advice and not a defence. Every decision to take or to decline a shot is yours alone, and you remain responsible for your backstop, your range estimate, your competence at that range, and the outcome. If the numbers and the animal in front of you disagree, do not shoot.

The app generates documents such as Trained Hunter Declarations, Game Dealer Consignment Declarations, and Larder Books from the information you enter. These documents record declarations you are making; they are not issued, certified, or endorsed by us. You are responsible for the accuracy of every entry and declaration, and for meeting any regulatory obligations that apply to you (including but not limited to assimilated Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 and the UK Game Regulations).
