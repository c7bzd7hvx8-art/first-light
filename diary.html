Edit display name

Used on your account card, in PDF signatures, and alongside your entries in any syndicate you belong to. Changing it updates those places only — existing shooter names on past diary entries are left as they were.

Season start month

Your diary year runs 12 months from this month. It sets how your entries, stats, targets and exports are grouped into seasons. Syndicate views are not affected — they follow each syndicate’s own setting.

Deer on my ground

Tick the deer species that live on your ground. The forecast only adds a rut boost for species you actually have — so a Roe-only ground won’t be scored as busy during the autumn Red, Fallow & Sika rut. Leave all ticked for the general forecast.

Kept on this device only — it is not part of your account, so a new phone or browser starts again with all species ticked.

Carcass
Larder weight is the gralloched carcass as it goes into the chiller — head off, legs removed, in fur, the convention the Deer Initiative Partnership / BASC cull records guide asks for. Whichever way you weigh, weigh the same way every time: mixed conventions cannot be compared across a season, or against anyone else’s figures. Typical larder weights — roe 12, fallow 22, sika 24, red 47 kg.
